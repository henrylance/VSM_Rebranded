In Her Honour

The Woman Behind Every Word

Men have gone to war with pictures of their loved ones. For a moment of solace, they look inside their helmets. When death is close, it lingers on your lips.

You are my life. The light I wake up to every morning. The love we share is the reason these words lift off the pages. You are my solace and instead of a helmet, I use a wallet. In your eyes, I see a world which only ever lived in my books.

Without you, there is no VSM.

From the Poet

An Open Letter

Written over the ocean in the moonlight

Dear Carol,

Since my childhood, I have been able to pen down whatever I wished. I felt like an artist with a ballpen and a tissue paper. Somehow, somewhere along the way you managed to change it. You my dear, made a poet speechless.

There had a come a time, where I had given up on writing. My dream of becoming a poet was abandoned until you brought it back to me. I still don't believe even after all this time, I am still writing. I will never be able to thank you enough for this alone.

You are my VSM. And I hope this reaches the world, they learn about you. They admire your love and wish you well. VSM will always be ours, not just mine.

With all my love,

❧
